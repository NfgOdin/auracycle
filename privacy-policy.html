Privacy Policy for Aura: Cycle & Hormone Intelligence

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

Application: Aura: Cycle & Hormone Intelligence (Package: com.nfgodin.auracycle)

Our Absolute Privacy Guarantee: Aura is architected with a strict Zero-Cloud, 100% On-Device philosophy. Your cycle logs, symptoms, BBT, pregnancy milestones, and personal notes NEVER leave your physical phone and are NEVER transmitted to our servers or sold to any third party.

1. Personal Health and Cycle Data

All data you enter into Aura—including menstruation dates, flow intensity, basal body temperature (BBT), cervical fluid characteristics, moods, physical symptoms, medications, infant feeding times, and contraction logs—is stored in a local SQLite database encrypted with 256-bit AES SQLCipher directly on your Android device.

We do not operate backend servers that collect or aggregate user health records. You maintain 100% custody and control of your health data at all times.

2. Health Connect Integration (Optional)

If you explicitly grant Aura permission to connect with Android Health Connect, the app reads the following data points locally:

  • Basal Body Temperature: Used exclusively to plot morning temperature curves and detect biphasic ovulation shifts on your local device.
  • Sleep: Used to display sleep duration alongside cycle phases and postpartum recovery metrics.
  • Heart Rate: Used to correlate resting heart rate trends with hormonal phases on your device.

Health Connect data is read directly on-device and is never shared, transferred, or uploaded to any external server.

3. Advertising and Third-Party SDKs

Aura uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display non-intrusive advertisements. Google AdMob may collect certain device-level information as required to serve ads:

  • Device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID).
  • Coarse location or IP address for regional ad delivery.
  • Crash reports and app diagnostics.

Aura configures Google AdMob with restricted data processing parameters and never shares any health, cycle, or reproductive biomarkers with ad networks.

4. In-App Purchases and Billing

All financial transactions for Aura Pro Lifetime and visual customizations are processed securely by Google Play In-App Billing. Aura does not collect, receive, or store your credit card details or billing address.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data is stored entirely on your device, you have immediate and permanent control over it:

  • In-App Data Erasure: You can erase all cycle, symptom, and maternal logs at any time via Settings > Erase All Data.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application immediately purges all encrypted local databases and application preferences from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Aura is designed for adults and young adults tracking their reproductive health (ages 16 and over). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. Medical Disclaimer

Aura is an educational tracking, cycle awareness, and mathematical estimation tool. It is not an FDA-certified medical device or diagnostic instrument. It should not be used as a standalone contraceptive method or as a substitute for professional clinical medical advice.
